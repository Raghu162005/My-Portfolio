01 — About

Engineer by training.
Builder by instinct.

The short version: I like problems that force me to understand the whole system, not just the screen.

I’m a Computer Science student who likes the space where clean interfaces meet solid engineering.

I study at Bapuji Institute of Engineering and Technology, Davangere (B.E. CSE, 2023–2027). Most of what I know has come from building things and then figuring out why they broke.

My work spans full-stack platforms, applied AI and secure systems: a Docker-sandboxed coding judge, a citation-validating research agent, a face-verified voting platform. Different problems, same instinct — go one layer deeper than the tutorial.

I approach problems by modelling the data first, shipping the smallest proof, then tightening the edges. I’m looking for a product-minded engineering team where I can own features end to end and grow alongside people who care about craft.
